Executive Summary

The Equinix Customer Portal (ECP) is intended to serve as our central hub for customers—a primary destination where they can easily make purchases, address issues, and configure their implementations with ease.

However, users of the ECP encounter difficulties in effectively managing orders, reviewing billing and inventory, resolving problems, and deploying their implementations. These challenges result in longer time to value (TTV), delays in revenue generation, and an overall unsatisfactory customer experience.

A disjointed and ineffective customer portal leads to operational inefficiencies, requiring our account and support teams to bridge the gaps. Additionally, managing multiple portals drives up our IT maintenance costs.

Our vision is ECP is a unified platform that empowers users with seamless access, actionable insights, and comprehensive control. It enables them to effortlessly purchase, resolve issues, and configure their implementations with ease.


Vision

Equinix Customer Portal is a unified platform that empowers users with seamless access, actionable insights, and comprehensive control. It enables them to effortlessly purchase, resolve issues, and configure their implementations with ease.


Problem

Enterprise customers like Vanguard, Norges Bank and AT&T desire a unified and intuitive self-serve experience. Our ECP product strategy addresses the following portal limitations:

•	Fragmented Support experiences causing long response times
•	Lack of a single, comprehensive view of all support tickets
•	Limited Product Discovery and Personalization
•	Inefficient and Overly Complex Transactions
•	Poor user onboarding experience
•	Broken organization setup and access controls
•	Lack of proactive communication for delays


Objective

The objective of the pilot is to deliver the following benefits:

•	Increased Customer Satisfaction & Retention
•	Operational Efficiency & Cost Reduction
•	Faster Time-to-Value 
•	Revenue Growth & Upsell Opportunities
